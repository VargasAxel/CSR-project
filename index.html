Who We Are

A Purpose-Driven Consultancy Built for Impact

PHI Resilience Strategic Management Services is a Philippine-based consultancy designing and implementing solutions for CSR, sustainability, and organizational resilience. We partner with businesses, nonprofits, and local governments to address climate change, disaster risk, inequality, and community vulnerability.

We specialize in integrating risk-informed CSR, early action protocols, pre-arranged financing, and innovative technologies — ensuring communities are protected before disaster strikes. Our interdisciplinary expertise bridges corporate strategy and humanitarian impact.

🌿 ESG-Aligned 🇵🇭 Philippines-Based 📊 UN SDG Benchmarked 🛰️ Tech-Enabled 🤝 Multi-Sector

How We Collaborate

Our 5-Point Partnership Model

PHI Resilience thrives on collaboration with corporations, NGOs, governments, donor agencies, and academic institutions — ensuring alignment, co-creation, and genuine capacity-building of local actors.

1

Strategic Alignment with Partner Goals

All collaborations aligned with mission and priorities of each partner.

  • Corporations: ESG compliance, employee engagement, sustainability goals
  • Foundations & Bilateral Agencies: Evidence-based impact, scalability, SDG alignment
  • NGOs & Academic Institutions: Co-create on research, implementation, and training
2

Co-Design and Advisory Support

A participatory approach to every stage of program development.

  • Diagnostics and readiness assessments to shape tailored CSR roadmaps
  • Co-creates resilience initiatives — livelihoods, business continuity, nature-based solutions
  • Integrates ESG reporting metrics to communicate impact clearly and credibly
3

Capacity Building for Local Partners

Empowering grassroots implementers to sustain programs independently.

  • Training barangay-level implementers, school leaders, and civic organizations
  • Hazard mapping, green technology tools, and community organizing support
  • Developing local champions who carry program momentum beyond the partnership
4

Knowledge Sharing & Resource Leveraging

Mobilizing a diverse network for shared learning and co-financing.

  • Multi-sector learning exchanges, public-private dialogues, and community impact labs
  • Access to policy frameworks, training modules, and ESG compliance tools
  • Shared funding models for co-financing climate and development projects
5

Program Replication and Scaling

Proven pilots packaged for easy replication across communities.

  • Urban Garden-to-Table, Safe Learning Spaces, Micro-enterprise Risk Readiness — documented for replication
  • Partners benefit from co-branding, shared knowledge capital, and expanded geographic reach
  • Implementation guides, monitoring frameworks, and coaching for multi-site rollout
